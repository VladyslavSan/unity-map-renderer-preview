unity-map-renderer

A Unity-native renderer for MapLibre-style vector maps, built on Burst and the job system. This preview runs it in your browser on WebGPU.

Open the map Watch the flythrough

Each player downloads about 18 MB. On the first visit the page installs a service worker and reloads once.

What it does

  • MapLibre styles, vector tilesReads MapLibre style JSON and Mapbox Vector Tiles: background, fill, line, symbol and fill-extrusion layers, with expressions and filters.
  • 3D buildingsFill-extrusion buildings with sun shadows and ambient occlusion.
  • LabelsText and icon symbols at points and along lines, placed with collision detection every frame.
  • Globe and MercatorThe same map on a globe or on a flat Web Mercator plane.
  • Sky, sun and time of daySky and fog from the style, and a sun whose position follows the time of day. Liberty Night is a night variant of the style.
  • Unity-native, data-orientedTile decode and meshing run as Burst jobs. Tiles draw through Entities, BatchRendererGroup or GameObjects.

Browser requirements

The players need WebGPU and a reasonably capable GPU.

  • Recommended: a current Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer.
  • Safari 26 and newer, and Firefox where it has WebGPU on, may also work.
  • Chrome and Firefox on Linux do not turn WebGPU on by default today.
  • Phones and tablets are not a target of this preview.

Use the styles

The two styles the players use, as raw MapLibre style JSON. They load tiles, sprites and glyphs from OpenFreeMap.

Liberty

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VladyslavSan/unity-map-renderer-preview/main/styles/liberty.json

The OpenFreeMap Liberty style, unmodified.

Liberty Night

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VladyslavSan/unity-map-renderer-preview/main/styles/liberty-night.json

Liberty with darker colours and top-level light and sky blocks, made for this renderer.
